# ZENOX JUST FOR YOU - Gaming Store Website

## Overview

**ZENOX JUST FOR YOU** is a South Africa based gaming top-up and digital products store designed to provide gamers with a simple and attractive way to purchase game-related products.

The website focuses on:
- Diamonds Top Ups
- Prime Store Gifts
- Memberships
- Prime Points

The design uses a modern blue and white theme with a light interface and animated features.
